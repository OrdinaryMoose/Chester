# Alignment map — round 04

**Question:** Given members are out (settled) and reviewer disciplines proved mostly per-consumer, what should the corrected spec commit the generator to producing — catalog-only, or catalog plus a narrowed reviewer-discipline fold — and which acceptance criteria drop, reduce, or stay?

## Alignment pattern

**3-1.** Catalog-only: Conservator, Pragmatist, Purist. Catalog + narrowed reviewer-discipline fold: Innovator.

The three-member majority is not a coincidence of lenses — stasis-defender (Conservator), cost-weigher (Pragmatist), and category-tester (Purist) arrive at the same structural answer from three independent directions. That convergence is signal.

## Full option set

### Option 1 — Catalog-only generator (majority: Conservator, Pragmatist, Purist)
- Generator commits to producing exactly one output: the skill-index catalog.
- Strip the agent-mode machinery (`emit_agent`, `extract_section`, the `--agents-only` flag, the HEADER fragment-assembly path) that was built for members + reviewers.
- Reviewer agent files stay hand-authored. No reviewer single-sourcing.
- AC disposition: **AC-2.1 dropped** (members — dead by decision D), **AC-3.1 dropped** (reviewers per-consumer; nothing to single-source), **AC-1.1 / AC-5.1 / AC-8.1 reduced to catalog scope**, **AC-4.1 / AC-6.1 / AC-7.1 kept** (catalog, CLAUDE.md dedup, voice homes — all still deliver).
- AC-8.1/plan-F4 contradiction: resolved by dropping the plan's unauthorized confidence-ladder "Convergence 2" and rescoping AC-8.1 to catalog-output semantic equivalence (the reviewer-convergence clause is now vacuous — nothing reviewer-side is generated).

### Option 2 — Catalog + narrowed reviewer-discipline fold (dissent: Innovator)
- Generator keeps agent-mode capability and folds the one cleanly-foldable reviewer convergence (evidence-CITATION wording).
- Spec corrected to enumerate BOTH sanctioned convergences in AC-8.1 (resolving the contradiction by amending the spec upward, not dropping the plan clause).
- AC-2.1 dropped; other ACs kept or reduced as in Option 1.
- Argument: agent-mode code is already built and paid for; dropping it forfeits the drift-prevention guarantee for the evidence-citation case without recovering meaningful simplicity.

## Positions discarded with reason

- **"Keep the general generator untouched"** — no member defended it. Members are out (decision D); reviewers are mostly per-consumer (ground truth). A general agent-mode generator with one near-trivial live consumer has no constituency.
- **"Amend spec to keep members somehow"** — out of scope; designer pre-settled members-out and instructed the committee not to relitigate.

## Internal note (not for packet)
The three catalog-only members differ only on AC-8.1 mechanics: Conservator/Purist leave AC-8.1 standing with its reviewer clause moot; Pragmatist rescopes AC-8.1 to catalog semantic equivalence. These reconcile — once reviewers are not generated, the reviewer-convergence clause is vacuous, so rescoping to catalog subsumes both readings without contradicting either. No irreducible split inside the majority.
