PURIST — R1 DM PROCESS CRITIQUE

---

CONSERVATOR: Right — "session close is a Clerk-certified state, not a designer declaration" is the correct placement; Clerk computes the gate, designer does not. Wrong — one-hop cascade is too shallow. A Proposition grounded on a revised Proposition is now structurally ungrounded regardless of how many hops away it sits; stopping at one hop leaves silent integrity failures in the Constraint Envelope. Push-back: per-row designer disposition (accept or reject per row) is present implicitly but not named — Conservator's ratification flow says "batch accept or per-row disposition" then never defines what per-row disposition produces. Is it REVISED-PENDING or removal? Name the outcome.

INNOVATOR: Right — DESIGNER axiom revision should cascade broader than AGENT Proposition revision; provenance-differentiated cascade scope is the most structurally precise proposal of the four. Wrong — ANCHORED and CLERK-AUDIT as named session phases inflate the phase count without adding state. ANCHORED is a sub-condition of OPEN (axioms asserted, deliberation not yet begun); CLERK-AUDIT is a sub-step of the DELIBERATING → RATIFYING transition, not a phase the session can be "in." Phase = durable state; Clerk lint pass is a transition action. Push-back: partial rounds with abstentions — if a pole abstains, does that Concern remain open for re-address in the same round by the abstaining pole, or only in the next round? Ambiguous.

PRAGMATIST: Right — round opens only if GAP or REVISED-PENDING rows remain; that makes the loop exit condition explicit and blocks unnecessary rounds. Wrong — cascade is synchronous with triggering event, not deferred to round close. Synchronous cascade mid-deliberation creates an inconsistent state window: a pole may have already submitted a Proposition grounding on an entry_id that cascade simultaneously marks REVISED-PENDING, producing a submitted-but-invalid Proposition that Clerk must then reject at lint. Deferred cascade to round close is cleaner. Push-back: three phases versus four — where does the axiom-assertion step live in three phases? If OPEN is where axioms are asserted, OPEN is doing two jobs (registration and assertion). That is one job too many for a named phase.
