## ADD

- Enumeration density for the three fields is unspecified. The body field says "IF/THEN" but does not close the vocabulary of what may appear as the THEN clause. Without a bounded clause-type list (e.g., "architectural commitment sentence," not free prose), the field is syntactically constrained but semantically open. That gap will be exploited under time pressure.
- Cascade invalidation protocol says "rules.md must specify" but framing does not specify what triggering event re-opens a ratified Concern. The Purist position: the trigger must be a closed-set condition (e.g., Evidence ID cited by a ratified body is revised or withdrawn), not a judgment call left to the designer.
- Coverage map derivation from schema fill is asserted but the mapping rule is implicit. Framing says "if schema fill does not yield all three by read, schema fails" — that is a test, not a specification. The Clerk needs an explicit coverage-map construction rule: which fields contribute to which artifact and how.
- Axiom-assertion mechanism (D2) is listed as open but framing does not constrain the candidate space to closed-set options. Three candidates are named (Register entry, tool call, session-open statements) but the framing does not say what structural property an acceptable mechanism must have. Without that, D2 adjudication is unconstrained and any choice passes.

## VETO

Nothing structurally vetoed. However: the "Arbiter (consultant)" role entry in Pole Roles is a category boundary violation. Arbiter-as-consultant is a hybrid that dissolves the Clerk/Arbiter distinction the AoA established. The framing names "Clerk replaces Arbiter" in the Vision section but then reinstates Arbiter as a named role in a softer capacity. Pick one: Arbiter is gone (Clerk replaces fully), or Arbiter is retained with a hard-bounded charter. The current framing has both, which introduces an unclosed role enumeration.

---

## RISKS

- **Vocabulary bleed risk.** IF/THEN body with no clause-type enumeration is one drift cycle from prose. The original proof system's Proposition schema had typed sub-fields (action label, grounding chain, reasoning chain). Collapsing to "IF/THEN free text" removes those typed sub-fields. If the body's THEN clause is unconstrained, the agent will fill it with implementation vocabulary that passes syntactic lint but violates altitude. The Clerk's lint regex cannot catch this without a keyword blocklist and a permitted-clause-type enumeration.
- **Three-artifact boundary underspecification.** Constraint envelope and resolution criterion are defined by what they contain; coverage map is defined by what it points to. But the field-to-artifact mapping is not stated. Any audit of "did this session produce all three artifacts" requires a construction rule the framing does not supply. This creates an exit-condition ambiguity that Clerk and designer will resolve inconsistently across sessions.
- **"Word-limit exempt" schema scope is unbounded.** Framing grants schema/ and design-brief-template.md unlimited length. Without a structural bound on schema enumeration growth, the closed-set discipline the Purist defends can be diluted through schema expansion without designer decision. Need a gate: schema vocabulary additions require explicit designer ratification, not agent self-expansion.

Everything else is buildable under current framing with rules.md carrying the cascade and coverage-map construction specs.
