CONSERVATOR: Right — team-lead dispatches rounds and produces session-close artifact set; scoping team-lead as coordination + extraction with no deliberative surface is correct role discipline. The LLM Clerk's collision judgment is non-deterministic: same body pair may yield different PASS/BLOCK on different runs, which makes the structural gate unreliable. Push-back: Submit Round actor = TEAM-LEAD "on designer authorization" introduces an unspecified authorization mechanism. What is the form of designer authorization — a named procedure, a verbal signal, a delegation protocol? If unspecified, Clerk cannot enforce it and the round-close authority boundary is fuzzy.

That means team-lead's dispatch is an out-of-band action invisible to the procedure set. Should it be a named procedure with team-lead as actor, or is informal dispatch genuinely sufficient?

Researcher is always available; "consultable" is the correct framing, not "attendee."
