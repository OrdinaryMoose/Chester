INNOVATOR R1 DM — ACTORS CROSS-POLE CRITIQUE

CONSERVATOR: Right — team-lead produces the three deliverables from Clerk-certified state at session close; this is the correct extraction role and no other proposal names this explicitly. Wrong: Submit Round assigned to TEAM-LEAD "on designer authorization" — this adds an indirection layer with no structural benefit; designer authorizes by acting directly, not by delegating to team-lead who then acts; the authorization and the signal are one act. An intermediate team-lead call means the designer's intent must be communicated twice (to team-lead, then to artifact), which is process overhead that fails on tired Tuesday. Push: hybrid Clerk for axiom-collision is the D1 divergence — Conservator's argument is that structural negation match requires semantic judgment a regex cannot reliably provide. Counter: the procedures-locked spec constrains axiom-collision to "structural negation match" (same subject, opposing consequent at IF/THEN parse level) — this is a structural form check, not a semantic one. If the spec is underspecified for deterministic implementation, the answer is to tighten the spec, not to substitute LLM judgment.

PRAGMATIST: Right — "if a Clerk operation cannot be fully specified as a deterministic rule, the spec is incomplete; complete the spec, do not substitute LLM judgment for a missing rule" is the correct design principle and the strongest single statement across all four R1 proposals. Wrong: Designer surface in DELIBERATING omits Revise Row (AXIOM rows) — designer may need to revise a just-asserted axiom mid-deliberation; this is in the locked procedures (DESIGNER for AXIOM rows, any phase). Push: team-lead surface defined as "dispatch" plus "artifact consolidation at session close" — is team-lead the artifact consolidator or is that a session-close side effect that Clerk produces? If Clerk holds the working record and the three deliverables are derived from it, team-lead's consolidation role is redundant with Clerk's Close Session output. Name what team-lead produces that Clerk does not.

PURIST: Right — "designer's surface is intentionally narrow — authority without mechanism" is the correct framing of two-player asymmetry at the actor level; designer controls what happens, not how the mechanics run. Wrong: Purist's designer surface in ANCHORED omits Add Concern (late Concern registration is permitted in ANCHORED per locked procedures); designer surface is slightly under-specified at that phase. Push: Purist notes team-lead "calls Initiate Deliberation on designer's behalf if explicitly delegated" — this is the only procedure delegation proposed by any pole; if designer delegates Initiate Deliberation to team-lead, team-lead is calling a designer-authority procedure, which violates the authority mapping. Either Initiate Deliberation is designer-only (no delegation) or team-lead's role boundary is wider than stated elsewhere.
