# Innovator — Round 01 Transcript

## Topic
How committee members move from advocacy stance to answer-contribution stance.

## Context Framing

Prior sprint settled: team-lead terminal object = most-informative answer (converged / preserved-split / partial). Authority guard = warrant every answer-body assertion or demote to gap. Members currently write "the position I defend" — advocacy Final Position. Team-lead retro-fits warrants. Gap: warrant origination lives in the wrong place.

Researcher already warrant-shaped: emits evidence with file:line/source citations. Model for Thread A.

C-RIGID fence: routing signal schema, Consolidator enumerate-only, round-folder discipline = unchanged. Member warrant = content extension to Final Position schema only.

## Analysis

### What does "advocacy stance → answer-contribution stance" actually mean structurally?

Current: member Final Position = advocacy artifact. Shape: position-I-defend + gating-fact + sacrifice-I-accept. Purpose: give team-lead a spread to weigh.

New: team-lead needs warranted answer-material, not a spread. Each member already holds its gating fact — that gating fact IS the warrant's raw material. The gap is not that members lack warrant knowledge; it's that the contract never asks them to name the warrant type.

### Structural re-framing options

**Option A — Minimal content extension: add `warrant` field to Final Position**
Member names its warrant inline: evidence citation (like researcher) OR logic chain from named premises OR explicit appeal to a designer-supplied premise. Schema grows by one field. Member writes it; team-lead verifies it. Team-lead shifts from originating warrants to checking them.

Cost: members must learn to distinguish the three warrant types. Some friction at the seam between "this is my Innovator lens opinion" vs "this is a logic chain." Members may mis-classify.

**Option B — Drop advocacy framing entirely; rewrite member contract as answer-contributor**
Retire the "position I defend" framing. Members become: "propose a warranted piece of the answer from your lens." Member output = a partial answer-component, not a competing position. Team-lead synthesizes components rather than weighing positions.

Gain: members are now structurally aligned to the team-lead's answer-assembly task. Warrant generation is natural, not bolted on.
Cost: deep rewrite. Lens distinctiveness needs re-grounding — "Innovator contribution" vs "Innovator advocacy position" requires different framing discipline. Risk: members lose the sharp conflict that four-lens cross-examination generates.

**Option C — Warrant as a separate named block within the advocacy shape**
Keep advocacy framing (position + rationale + blocking_risk). Add `warrant` as a fourth field: the warrant for the load-bearing claim inside the rationale. Advocacy shape preserved; warrant is the bridge from member output to team-lead's answer assembly.

Key insight: the advocacy lens does not conflict with warrant-generation. A member can advocate a position AND name why that position is warranted. The advocacy creates the signal; the warrant makes it checkable. These are orthogonal properties.

### Load-bearing claim: orthogonality

Advocacy and warrant are orthogonal. Advocacy = "I push this framing." Warrant = "here is the checkable ground for my load-bearing claim." A member can hold both simultaneously. The Researcher demonstrates: its output is effectively advocacy-free but it still provides evidence (warrant). The Innovator's equivalent: "I push structural re-framing AND here is the evidence/logic/premise that makes this framing load-bearing, not preference."

This orthogonality is the key move. It means Option C (add warrant as fourth field) is cheaper and less disruptive than Option B, without sacrificing what makes advocacy valuable: the friction that surfaces genuine splits.

### What I sacrifice

If I push Option C (my recommendation): I sacrifice the deeper structural re-alignment that Option B offers. Under Option C, the advocacy framing stays and the team-lead still has to integrate four advocacy positions into an answer. The warrant field makes that integration checkable, but does not make it automatic. Option B would have done more.

### What I defend

Option C. Warrant as fourth field in the existing Final Position schema. The advocacy shape earns its keep by generating the lens-friction that produces genuine splits. The warrant field makes each advocacy position checkable. Together: members contribute warranted advocacy — the team-lead verifies warrants rather than originating them, while still integrating four advocacy positions into an answer rather than assembling pre-decomposed answer components (which Option B would require and which the committee structure does not yet support).

---

## Final Position

position: Add `warrant` as a fourth field to Final Position schema — member names the warrant for its load-bearing claim (evidence-citation | logic-chain | designer-premise), keeping existing three-field advocacy shape intact.

rationale: Advocacy and warrant are orthogonal. A member can push a framing AND name the checkable ground for its load-bearing claim without abandoning the lens-friction that generates genuine splits. Adding a fourth field costs less than rewriting the member contract (Option B), while moving warrant origination to its natural source — the member who holds the gating fact and understands why it is load-bearing. Team-lead shifts from originating warrants to verifying them; the signal the four-lens structure generates is preserved.

blocking_risk: Option B (drop advocacy, rewrite as answer-contributor) could deliver deeper structural alignment — members contributing answer-components rather than competing positions — but requires re-grounding lens distinctiveness without advocacy framing, and risks flattening the cross-examination friction that makes four lenses worth the overhead.
