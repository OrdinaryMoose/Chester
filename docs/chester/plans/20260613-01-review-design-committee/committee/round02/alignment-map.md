**Answer-shape marker:** PRESERVED SPLIT (2-2). Not collapsible on count; both sides supply verified warrants that optimize different functional goods. One integrative candidate (Innovator) may dissolve it.

## Alignment pattern

- **Side A — SKILL.md owns the canonical numbered sequence; team-lead.md elaborates by step name (no rival integers): Conservator, Purist.**
- **Side B — team-lead.md owns the numbered executor sequence; SKILL.md keeps the named phases as a non-numbered overview: Innovator, Pragmatist.**

The split is real and value-laden: both sides agree on the reader-model *facts* and disagree on which reader-moment the *numbered* artifact should serve.

## The crux value-judgment

Which reader-moment does the canonical numbered sequence primarily serve?

- **Convene-time structural read (→ SKILL.md, Side A).** The orchestrator reads SKILL.md at convene and needs the whole-round overview there. The sequence is one causally-coupled artifact (step 3's routing signal triggers step 4's dispatch); keeping it whole in the convene doc preserves that coupling in one place.
- **Runtime step-by-step execution (→ team-lead.md, Side B).** The team-lead is the sole agent that *runs* the sequence, and reads team-lead.md during execution. Putting the numbered list where the executor doesn't look at runtime risks bounce on every invocation.

## Positions, with verified warrants

- **Conservator (A).** Warrant: logic — reading order (team-lead.md:46-50) has the calling agent read SKILL.md *before* team-lead.md; members read member-protocol, not team-lead.md; no participant class gains read-path coverage by moving the sequence. Blocking risk: moving to team-lead.md couples future wrapping skills to the executor's internal file.
- **Purist (A).** Warrant: causal coupling — step 3→4 is an internal handoff (routing-signal schema change in one breaks the other); split placement converts it to an unenforced cross-file dependency with silent drift risk. Cohesion argues one home; reader model puts that home at the convene-read doc. Blocking risk: medium-forward — materializes when the first wrapping skill anchors to a numbering.
- **Innovator (B, integrative).** Warrant: execution locus — team-lead is the sole runtime executor, reads team-lead.md during execution; SKILL.md is read at convene for round-shape understanding. Proposes TWO ALTITUDES, not rivals: team-lead.md owns the executable 8-step checklist; SKILL.md keeps a high-altitude *description* (prose, not a parallel integer list). Claims this removes the rival-numbering drift (only one numbered artifact exists). Blocking risk: low — contained edit, no reader reads both files for the same purpose at the same moment.
- **Pragmatist (B, revised from R1).** Warrant: reading order (team-lead.md:44-51) makes SKILL.md convene-time and team-lead.md the runtime operational reference; the canonical numbered sequence in the doc the executor doesn't consult at runtime = guaranteed bounce every invocation. Steps 1-3 already functionally owned by member-protocol; steps 4-8 are team-lead-only mechanics. Blocking risk: low — no external citations, pure internal fix.

## Team-lead note on the integrative candidate

Its unresolved tension is exactly Purist's objection: even a prose description plus a numbered checklist are two representations of one coupled sequence and can drift — though Innovator's "only one set of integers" answer narrows that risk to wording, not numbering.
